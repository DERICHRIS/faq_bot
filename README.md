AI/ML Engineer — Conversational AI Chatbot Fine-tuning (Mental Health Project)
We are seeking a highly motivated AI/ML Engineer to join our mission-driven team, focused on building and fine-tuning conversational AI models for mental health applications. You will be at the core of enhancing empathetic, safe, and highly responsive AI chatbots that can support users dealing with mental health challenges.

Responsibilities:
Fine-tune large language models (e.g., GPT, LLaMA, Mistral) for safe, empathetic mental health conversations.

Implement RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) and other fine-tuning techniques to align model outputs with clinical and ethical guidelines.

Preprocess, clean, and curate specialized mental health conversational datasets while ensuring data privacy and compliance (HIPAA/GDPR if needed).

Work closely with clinical experts and mental health professionals to define safety filters, response guardrails, and escalation policies for AI interactions.

Optimize models for low-latency, real-time conversations without sacrificing accuracy or empathy.

Integrate external APIs (e.g., crisis support helplines, emergency response triggers) into the chatbot framework.

Conduct bias audits, safety evaluations, and user testing to iteratively improve the chatbot.

Deploy models in a scalable environment (AWS, Azure, GCP) and maintain continuous model improvement pipelines.
